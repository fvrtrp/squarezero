It's been 10 years since stepped foot in Florida, and started College. 2016. August 12 - can't believe it's been that long.
Feels like it was just yesterday when I took the bus from Orlando onto Gainesville. Met Kanna, Yuvi, Aditya.
Reitz Union. Gator Fridays. All the Nachos. All the free movies screenings. All the bowling days. The times the world felt truly at my grasp. When I felt the most alive.
That was an inflection point that my future hinged on.
Felt wanted. Felt young. Felt powerful. The chance encounter in Reitz Union when I didn't take a step. The chance encounter on the bus to University Commons. The other chance encounter on the bus from University Commons.
If I had only taken step, atleast one step. 
But I had some, and those always turned out sweet. The Thanksgiving dinner at Mr. K's house, volunteering for Gator's for Asha, all the late nights spent on phone talking with my heart racing.
What was the feeling? How did I feel that? What's happened now? Why don't I feel like that anymore?

I've realized I've always been quixotic with my ideas but I was never one for execution. I've had grand ideals and delusions but I was never one for follow-up. And looking back at myself, I have never been that person that I thought I was.
Never. I was never gonna catch up. But that actually makes me feel better because it's not like I've fallen off a cliff: I was never on the cliff. I was looking up from the abyss, being delulu.
But man, I miss all those times. I miss my youth. I'm 31 now. Fuck, that's old. And again, it's the same thing of it's now or never. It's always been now or never. 

I miss dreaming. I used to dream things that would make my heart race. Dreams caused yearning, longing and a craving for something. It moved me. I think it's part of soul dissonance.
I don't know exactly what it is, maybe it's all the constant ingress of all the information slop brainrot chaos, or the mundanity of everyday life that isn't so interesting anymore, 
but I'm not with my soul. I haven't been myself in a long time. Actually, the more I think about it, I've never really been one with my real self.
There's my idealist lazy daydreaming behemoth that is leagues far from the real me thrown into the world. The real me is just going through the motions; the reigns are slack and it's drifting amok with no purpose or place.
What the hell? Why don't I connect with my real self? I need to have stakes in this, it's my life! I have all my stakes here. I've got to keep shit together. I've got to put out the burning fires, and I got to make things better: it's for myself!

It's not for someone else - it's for myself, isn't it really that simple? All the times I fucked up, I fucked up.
It's got to be vices and dopamine that's got me f'ed up after slowly but surely losing grip. I started losing grip during master's itself. I just wasn't aware enough. I didn't take chances and steps.

To keep things simple, here's what I got to do right now-
 - You're going to switch jobs. You're going to find something that will take you on a wild journey; it doesn't matter how hard you need to work for it, you will do it.
 - You'll be aware right here, right now. You'll show up at work, you'll learn all the things there are to learn and be aware of. You will practice, you will not chill out.
 - You'll think about yourself, and the way you eat, live, dress up, talk, love. You'll show up, because it matters.
 - You'll NOT get dragged into any kind of addiction. If something is making you lose control, just lie down. Take rest. Take a walk. Do nothing.
 DO NOT ROT. This includes gaming, eating, and all the other shitty vices you've so dearly welcome into your life. It's a shame, really.
 - Morals above all else. Be true to yourself. Be proud, and do the walk, too. Just need to do these. Nothing else, really.
 Don't expect immediate returns. But yes, there will be returns. Gratifying ones. Imagine the day you are awed by your reflection. Imagine when you get the job you always wanted. imagine the gains of slowly taking care of your home and seeing it improve day after day.
 imagine all the places you could travel to. 
 - You'll never lose sight of things. If things are going adrift, again just take a break. Don't commit to anything. Just show up.
